## The signature keynote

### Don’t Let Your Phone Upgrade Before You Do

People upgrade their phones every year without a second thought — but rarely apply that same intentionality to their own growth. This keynote uses that idea as a mirror: what would change if your team invested in themselves the way they invest in their devices?

Audiences leave with a practical, three-part framework for closing the gap between what they know and how they perform — and a renewed sense of ownership over their own development.

Ideal for: conferences, all-hands meetings, leadership offsites
