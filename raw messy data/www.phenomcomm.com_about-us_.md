## Our Methodology

Informed by established research, two decades of talent development experience, and direct engagement with today’s workforce, PhenomComm’s approach goes beyond delivering information—it changes behavior.

Most training programs raise awareness. **Ours changes how people show up**: in meetings, on calls, under deadline, in front of customers—and the change holds after the workshop ends.

This is the foundation of **The Upgrade Framework™**—the methodology outlined in Paulette’s book, **_Don’t Let Your Phone Upgrade Before You Do_,** and expanded through PhenomComm’s organizational leadership programs. It’s built on a simple premise: when the person improves, performance follows. Through a combination of proven frameworks, practical tools, and intentional application, we don’t just train teams—we upgrade people.

## Our Founder

Paulette has trained and coached employees across LaGuardia Terminal B and JFK Terminals 4 & 8—spanning customer service, retail, food and beverage, wheelchair services, maintenance, security, and airline operations. She knows what it takes to get consistent performance from hundreds of people, across shifts, under real-world pressure, with zero room for “we’ll fix it later.”

PhenomComm brings 20 years of hands-on talent development experience to every engagement, led by founder Paulette Cunningham.

Her background spans teaching and learning, instructional design, and professional development as an independent contractor—giving her a deep, ground-level understanding of human behavior across roles, industries, and walks of life. That understanding informs every solution PhenomComm designs, delivers, and measures.

Paulette is an award-winning former public school educator, where she learned a core truth that still drives her work today: people rise fast when they’re seen, challenged, and given real structure—not just encouragement. She’s spent two decades proving that principle holds true in classrooms, nonprofits, and high-traffic operational environments alike.

Colleagues from her airport operations career describe her as a strategic, forward-thinking professional who delivers results while elevating everyone around her.

Paulette is the author of **Don’t Let Your Phone Upgrade Before You Do**, the book that gave rise to The Upgrade Framework™. It’s the same core challenge she’s spent two decades solving in classrooms, airports, and boardrooms: people often pursue external upgrades before investing in their own growth.
