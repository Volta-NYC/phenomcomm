Technology keeps advancing. You don't.

Every year, your phone becomes faster, smarter and more capable. But personal growth doesn't happen with the push of a button. Without intention, it's easy to stay stuck running the same habits, beliefs, and patterns year after year.

In Don't Let Your Phone Upgrade Before You Do, keynote speaker Paulette Cunningham, M.Ed. introduces The Upgrade Framework™-a practical, 3-step process for growing as deliberately as the technology in your pocket: Acknowledge your Version Milestones, Decide on your Feature Selections (and lose your Legacy Bugs), and Match your outer world to your inner vision by building an Ecosystem that grows you instead of crushing you.

Drawing on a workshop delivered to thousands of professionals across industries—from frontline teams to leadership programs—this book gives you a simple, repeatable roadmap you can apply to your career, your relationships, or any area of your life that's ready for its next upgrade.

Inside, you'll learn how to:

- Identify your Version Milestones and leverage the proof that you've upgraded before
- Make deliberate Feature Selections and eliminate the Legacy Bugs holding you back
- Design an Ecosystem of Oak Tree Growers (and remove the Acorn Crushers) so your environment supports who you're becoming

Complete with real-world case studies, guided journaling prompts, a troubleshooting guide for common roadblocks, and a quick-reference glossary, this is your guide to upgrading with purpose-before the world upgrades without you.

Plus: Get free access to a Companion Workbook to help you put The Upgrade Framework™ into practice in your own life.

Your phone gets better every year. What about you?

Visit www.PhenomComm.com to learn more about The Upgrade Framework™ keynotes and training programs.
